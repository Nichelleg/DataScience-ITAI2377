## Featured Project: The XLE Recovery Play

**Group Lab 03 | Completed**  
**Project:** Drawing Insights from Data to Make Money Decisions  
**Team:** Crystal Insight

### Project Overview

Developed an evidence-based investment proposal examining
potential recovery opportunities following the historic
collapse in crude oil prices during the COVID-19 pandemic.

The project evaluated five investment candidates to
determine which offered a reasonable combination of
correlation with oil prices, recovery potential, and
manageable investment risk.

### Data and Methodology

- Analyzed 815 matched trading days from 2017–2020.
- Collected WTI crude oil prices from EIA/FRED.
- Retrieved historical stock and fund prices from Nasdaq.
- Compared five investment candidates: BA, OXY, COP,
  FRO, and XLE.
- Aligned commodity and equity datasets by trading date.
- Applied linear regression and examined R² values.
- Compared overall relationships with pre-pandemic trends.
- Evaluated company-specific and sector-wide investment risks.

### Key Findings

ConocoPhillips demonstrated the strongest overall
regression relationship with crude oil prices (R² ≈ 0.69).

However, XLE showed a stronger relationship during the
more stable 2019 period and offered diversification
across multiple energy companies.

The team recommended a modest investment in XLE rather
than concentrating the Family Fund in a single company.

### Technical Skills Demonstrated

- Financial data procurement and validation
- Dataset alignment and preprocessing
- Regression analysis
- Scatterplot and trendline interpretation
- Comparative statistical analysis
- Risk assessment
- Avoiding look-ahead bias
- Translating analytical findings into business decisions

### Key Takeaway

A strong statistical relationship does not automatically
justify an investment decision.

Effective data science requires understanding the
underlying business context, evaluating risk, and
recognizing the limitations of statistical models.

### Project Deliverables

- Technical analysis and recommendation report
- Comparative regression workbook (Excel)
- Group presentation
